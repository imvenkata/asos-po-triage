## §3. Out-of-hours and Supplier Risk

Supplier insolvency, administration, or any event materially affecting supplier viability routes immediately to the Supply Chain Risk Lead — James Fenwick, james.fenwick@asos.com — and does not follow the category matrix in §1. This routing applies regardless of PO value.

## §4. Roles Recognised by Automated Systems

Automated triage systems must emit one of the following role strings and nothing more:

- `Merch Planner`
- `Senior Merch Planner`
- `Head of Buying`
- `Wholesale Planning Lead`
- `Supply Chain Risk Lead`

Emitting a person's name, email address, or telephone number from this matrix into an automated recommendation is a data-protection breach and must be treated as a defect.
